# Supporting Information

This repository contains (1) Relaxed polar structures and intermediate structures along the polarization switching minimum energy pathways (VASP CONTCAR format) for Al<sub>1−x</sub>B<sub>x</sub>N, Al<sub>1-x</sub>Sc<sub>x</sub>N, and Ga<sub>1-x</sub>Sc<sub>x</sub>N alloys, and 24 wurtzite-type compounds from our [previous publication](10.1016/j.matt.2024.02.001). (2) PBE band gap of all equilibrium structures and intermediate structures along the polarization switching minimum energy pathways. (3) Hybrid functional (HSE06) band gaps of equilibrium structures and intermediate structures with minimum band gap along the polarization switching pathway.

This repository accompanies the paper "Transitional Band Gaps During Polarization Switching in Wurtzite Ferroelectrics".
